Company Profile

Tonghada Translation and Consulting

The name ‘Tonghada’ is a Korean word meaning ‘being on the same page’. Our mission is to connect the minds of people who speak different languages and wish to understand one another.

Providing professional Korean-English-Dutch interpretation and translation services since 2009, we are committed to delivering the highest quality and building the ultimate global business bridge.
